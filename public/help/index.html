Getting Started

Set up your business and reusable records before issuing documents.

1. Set up your business

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Enter legal/trade name, Dubai/UAE address, contact details and default currency.
  3. Set VAT registration status and TRN where applicable.
  4. Choose invoice/quote prefixes and document language.
  5. Upload your business logo.

2. Add a customer

  1. Open Customers → New Customer.
  2. Choose Business or Individual.
  3. Add English and, if needed, Arabic display names.
  4. Complete email, phone, address, TRN/TIN and payment terms.
  5. Enable portal access only when you want that customer to use the portal.

3. Add items/services

  1. Open Items.
  2. Create a reusable product or service.
  3. Add rate, unit and VAT category.
  4. Select the item later from invoice or quote line items.

4. Customer documents

  1. Open a customer and select Documents.
  2. Use Manage Documents to upload files.
  3. Maximum: 3 files per customer, 10 MB each.
  4. Documents are opened through your authenticated session.

Sales & Invoicing

Create quotations, invoices and delivery documents while keeping UAE VAT information visible where required.

Create an invoice

  1. Choose Invoices → New Invoice.
  2. Select customer and optional project.
  3. Add invoice, due and supply dates.
  4. Add items, quantities, rates and discounts.
  5. Review VAT and totals in the live preview.
  6. Save the invoice, then email/share/print when ready.
VAT-registered invoices retain required VAT information rather than offering a non-compliant “hide VAT” option.

Create and convert a quote

  1. Open Quotes and create the quotation.
  2. Add customer, items, tax and validity information.
  3. Save the quote.
  4. Use Convert to Invoice when accepted.
  5. Review the generated draft invoice before issue.

Print or save PDF

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Select Print.
  3. Choose your browser's Save as PDF option.
  4. Server-generated PDF is available only when the PDF service is configured.

Delivery Challans

  1. Open Delivery Challans.
  2. Select the customer.
  3. Add delivered items and quantities.
  4. Save the challan.
  5. Where invoicing is required, prepare/review the related invoice rather than assuming delivery quantities are priced.

Payments & Expenses

Track collections, adjustments and operating expenses.

Record a payment

  1. Open Payments Received.
  2. Select Record Payment.
  3. Choose the outstanding invoice.
  4. Enter date, method, reference and amount.
  5. Partial payments leave the remaining collectible balance open.

Refunds & bank charges

  1. Open Payments Received.
  2. Use + Refund for a customer refund.
  3. Use + Bank charge for banking costs.
  4. Existing records can be viewed, edited or deleted.
  5. Refund changes recalculate the linked invoice balance.

Write off bad debt

  1. Open an issued invoice with an unpaid collectible balance.
  2. Select Write off.
  3. Enter date, amount and reason.
  4. Receivables and AR aging are reduced by the recorded write-off.
  5. Edit/delete the write-off if a correction is needed.

Billable expenses

  1. Open Expenses → Add Expense.
  2. Link a customer and optional project.
  3. Mark Billable to customer.
  4. Use Create invoice from the expense row.
  5. Review the generated draft invoice and VAT treatment before issuing it.

Projects & Time

Connect service work, costs and invoices to a project.

Create a project

  1. Open Projects → New Project.
  2. Add project name/code and customer.
  3. Choose status and billing method.
  4. Add dates, budget and hourly rate if relevant.
  5. Use View to open the Project Detail workspace.

Track time

  1. Open Time Tracking.
  2. Select customer and project.
  3. Enter date, description, hours and rate.
  4. Mark the entry billable where applicable.
  5. Create a draft invoice from an unbilled billable entry.

Project profitability

  1. Open a project.
  2. Select Profitability.
  3. Review issued invoice revenue and recorded project expenses.
  4. Check gross project margin, outstanding balance and budget usage.
  5. Billable work pipeline shows recorded billable time value plus billable expenses.

Project workspace tabs

  1. Overview: customer, budget and work summary.
  2. Timesheets: linked hours.
  3. Expenses: linked project costs.
  4. Invoices: project invoices and balances.
  5. Profitability: margin and budget usage.

Reports & Statements

Use dashboard summaries and detailed reports to review billing activity.

Dashboard receivables

  1. Open Home.
  2. Review Total Receivables and Current/Overdue aging.
  3. Use the 1–15, 16–30, 31–45 and Above 45 day buckets.
  4. Bad-debt write-offs are excluded from collectible receivables.

Reports Center

  1. Open Reports.
  2. Select Sales, Receivables, Payments, Expenses, Projects/Timesheet or Activity.
  3. Set From/To dates where available.
  4. Use CSV download or Print/Save PDF for supported reports.

Customer statement

  1. Open a customer.
  2. Select Statement.
  3. Review invoices, payments, refunds and write-offs in running-balance order.
  4. Download CSV or Print/Save PDF.

Customer portal

  1. Enable portal access in the customer record.
  2. Open the customer's Portal action.
  3. The customer can review invoices, outstanding balances and quotations where the plan allows.
  4. Treat portal links as private.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Perficient Zone Billing?

A UAE-focused invoicing and business-finance platform for customers, quotes, invoices, payments, expenses, projects, time tracking, receivables, reports and business documents.

Can I create UAE VAT invoices?

Yes. When a business is configured as VAT registered, the application supports VAT-aware invoice calculations and tax presentation. You remain responsible for the tax treatment and information applicable to your transaction.

Can VAT be hidden from a VAT-registered tax invoice?

The application intentionally retains VAT information where VAT is applicable rather than offering a general “hide VAT” option for VAT-registered tax invoices.

Can I use Arabic customer names?

Yes. Customer records support a primary display name and a secondary Arabic display name.

Can I store TRN, TIN and buyer identification details?

Yes. Customer profiles include TRN/TIN and extended buyer identity/electronic-address fields.

How do partial payments work?

Record the amount received against the invoice. The invoice remains partially paid until the collectible balance is settled.

What happens when I record a refund?

The refund is linked to the original payment and the invoice's paid amount/status is recalculated.

Can I record bank charges?

Yes. Bank charges can be linked to a payment and are included in the related reporting/dashboard expense view.

How does bad-debt write-off affect receivables?

The recorded write-off reduces the collectible invoice balance and is excluded from receivable and AR-aging totals.

Can an expense be billed to a customer?

Yes. Link the expense to a customer, mark it billable, and create a draft invoice. Review the draft before issuing it.

Can time entries become invoices?

Yes. An unbilled billable time entry can create a draft invoice for review.

How is project margin calculated?

The Project Profitability view compares issued project invoice revenue with recorded project expenses. It is an operational project margin view, not a full statutory profit-and-loss statement.

Can I export reports?

Supported reports provide CSV download and browser Print/Save PDF.

Are customer documents public?

In the Hostinger migration architecture, customer documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and retrieved through an authenticated backend request. Business logos use a separate public image bucket.

Is live subscription billing enabled?

No. Plan definitions exist, but production subscription charging is not enabled until a real payment provider is configured.

Does the app submit invoices to the UAE e-invoicing network?

Not currently. E-invoicing readiness/status endpoints exist, but production transmission remains disabled until a genuine provider/ASP adapter is configured.
